## Prompt đã dùng

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single clay-style 3D avatar for the personal software developer portfolio of Dao Van Huynh. Input 1 is the actual person's appearance reference. Input 2 is a material/rendering style reference ONLY (the four cartoon characters); do not reproduce any of those costumes or facial hair. Create ONE friendly young adult Vietnamese male avatar resembling the person in input 1: thick black side-parted hair swept softly to the side, clean-shaven, dark eyes and brows, no glasses, no hat. Warm subtle smile, approachable and thoughtful. Chest-up bust in a plain dark forest-green hoodie, over a cream T-shirt. Matte soft clay / high-end 3D emoji sculptural style, rounded simplified forms, careful detailed hair strands as sculpted broad shapes, natural warm skin tone matching input 1. Face three-quarter front, looking toward the viewer, comfortable relaxed pose. Large head and upper torso, centered, whole hair silhouette visible with generous padding, no crop of head, bust gently cropped below chest. Soft diffuse studio lighting from upper left, subtle contact and ambient shading on the subject. Truly transparent background, no backdrop, no ground plane, no other objects, no badges, no text, no logos, no watermark. This is a standalone asset, not a screenshot or collage.
